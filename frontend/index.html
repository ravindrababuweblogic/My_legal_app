      Your Basic Legal Rights

      🏠 Tenant Rights

      • Right to a habitable living environment
      • Right to privacy — landlord must give notice before entry
      • Protection against unlawful eviction
      • Right to return of security deposit within legal timeframe

      👷 Employment Rights

      • Right to minimum wage and overtime pay
      • Protection against workplace discrimination
      • Right to a safe working environment (OSHA)
      • Right to family and medical leave (FMLA)

      🚔 Civil Rights

      • Right to remain silent (5th Amendment)
      • Right to an attorney if arrested (6th Amendment)
      • Protection against unreasonable searches (4th Amendment)
      • Right to due process of law

      💼 Consumer Rights

      • Right to accurate billing and fair debt collection
      • Protection under consumer protection laws
      • Right to dispute credit report errors
      • Right to cancel certain contracts within 3 days
